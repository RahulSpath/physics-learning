The relations and typical numbers worth knowing by heart, from microelectronvolts to tens of electronvolts. SI units throughout.

How to use it: spend ten minutes a day on one section. Switch to Hide values or Hide relations, recall each entry, then tap it to check. When you miss one, derive it rather than rereading it.
